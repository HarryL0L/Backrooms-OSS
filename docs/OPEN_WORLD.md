# Open world: the city above the car park (idea, not started)

A Backrooms where you can drive out of the car park into an endless night
city in the rain, and every road brings you back.

## Story

- The Blue Hour is the car park's other side: car parks lie under cities, so
  if you come up the ramp you expect a city above. Real places stitched
  together wrongly, which is how the Backrooms work.
- An empty city at the hour nobody is awake is the biggest version of what
  the Backrooms copy: every window lit, nobody home. It looks like the place
  where you would find a way out, and you don't.
- Why drive: escape (the Attendant behind you, the keys in a car), and search
  (the city is too big to walk). The twist: however far you go, the roads
  loop back to the same crossing and the same failing lamp. The odometer
  says 40 km; you pass the same parked car again.
- It never stops raining in the Blue Hour, yet the sky is clear blue: no
  clouds anywhere.

## Stages

1. **Connection** (small): an exit ramp from the car park (barrier arm, EXIT
   sign) up onto the Blue Hour crossing; parked cars along the kerbs (the
   CC0 cars from assets/garage). The Attendant may follow you a little way
   up the ramp and stop at the edge of the street lights.
2. **Rain** (small/medium): rain streaks visible in the lamp light, ripples
   in the puddles (the wet streets of L.U.C.I.D. stage 4 already exist),
   drips from ledges and the fire stair, drops on the body cam lens, rain
   sound that changes under cover. Only drawn near the camera.
3. **Driving** (big): handling for the existing cars (they already have
   get in, seat and key), a larger drivable district with bounded roads.
4. **The loop** (medium): road layout that quietly stitches back to the same
   crossing; the speedometer and odometer give it away.
5. **Open world** (big): the city streamed in tiles like the Backrooms
   chunks, lit with L.U.C.I.D. stage 3's city-scale light management
   (instanced emitters, nearest real lights, baked tiles, day/night states).

See docs/LUCID.md for the lighting this relies on.
