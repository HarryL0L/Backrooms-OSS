# Car park assets

- `covered_car.glb`: "Covered Car" from Poly Haven, https://polyhaven.com/a/covered_car
  (authors: MP). License: CC0 1.0 (public domain). Reduced for the game
  (decimated to ~5k triangles, 1K textures).
- `suv.glb`, `suv_damaged.glb`: "Generic SUV ZYX0" by 1DInc, BlendSwap
  https://www.blendswap.com/blend/29795 (the "snow covered and wrecked"
  file, with a clean and a damaged car). License: CC0 1.0 (public domain).
  Snow and driver figure removed, decimated to ~37k triangles, flat
  materials; built with `tools/blender/suv_export.py`. No real make: its
  only lettering is "GENERIC".
