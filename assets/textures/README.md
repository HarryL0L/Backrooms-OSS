# Photo textures (Realistic preset)

Photographed materials for the Realistic graphics preset (the other presets
don't load them into their look). The game keeps its own colours and
patterns; the photo brings the real grain, fibres and relief. The ones here
and where they come from: see `LICENSE.md`. Supported files:

    carpet/albedo.jpg        carpet/normal.png
    wallpaper/albedo.jpg     wallpaper/normal.png
    ceiling_tile/albedo.jpg
    concrete/albedo.jpg      concrete/normal.png

Seamless (tileable) square images, 1024-2048 px. Normal maps in OpenGL
convention (green up). One repeat covers about 1.5 m of carpet, 1.2 m of
paper or tile and 2 m of concrete (see `world_materials.gd`).

**Licence: only CC0 (public domain) textures, e.g. ambientCG or Poly Haven,
or photos you took yourself.** Nothing that needs attribution or forbids
redistribution: this is an open source game.
