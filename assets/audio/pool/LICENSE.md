# Poolrooms voices

Field recordings from Freesound, each released under CC0 1.0 (public domain,
"Creative Commons 0" on the sound's page). Downloaded 2026-10-19 (the
high-quality previews), then: mono, 90 Hz-7 kHz, a little of a synthetic
1.6 s tiled-room reverb, loudness-normalised, 32 kHz Ogg Vorbis.

- `leisure.ogg`: "Swimming Pool Leisure Centre" by acollier123,
  https://freesound.org/people/acollier123/sounds/242959/ (CC0)
- `far.ogg`: "Water parc or public swimming pool changing area.wav" by BonnyOrbit,
  https://freesound.org/people/BonnyOrbit/sounds/645853/ (CC0)
